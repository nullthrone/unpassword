# unpassword mark

The mark is an open padlock drawn in the Nullthrone formal language:

- The **shackle** has stepped Art Deco shoulders, like the stepped head of the Nullthrone monogram. Its right leg is lifted out of the body: the lock is open.
- The **keyhole** is the monogram's arched "0" opening.
- The **cap bar** above the body and the slanted **side columns** repeat the throne silhouette.

| File | Use |
|---|---|
| `mark.svg` | Full mark, ≥ 48 px. Fills with `currentColor`. |
| `mark-small.svg` | Reduced mark without cap bar and columns, for ≤ 32 px (favicons, toolbar). `currentColor`. |
| `favicon.svg` | Small mark with built-in light/dark colours. |

Colours: ink, paper or brass only (see `design/nullthrone`). Keep a clear space of a quarter of the mark's width. PNG renderings (Marketplace icons, banner, social card, add-on logo) are generated by `scripts/render-brand-assets.mjs`.
